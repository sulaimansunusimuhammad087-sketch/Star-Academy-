About Star Academy

Star Academy is a premier community-based extra lesson program designed to rigorously prepare students for university admission and academic distinction. Operating every Saturday and Sunday from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, we train learners across Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels. Our core focus centers on mastery within the Science and Arts departments to secure successful higher education placement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our program? Find quick answers below.
How do I register as a new student?
Simply navigate to the Student Portal, click on the registration section, fill out your details, and your unique Student ID will be generated instantly!
How do I check my termly results?
Log into the Student Portal using your Student ID, Age, and Phone Number, then select your current session and term to view or download your report card.
When are classes held?
Classes run every Saturday and Sunday from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM at our community study center.
What subjects are covered?
We cover core subjects across both Science and Arts departments, ensuring thorough preparation for secondary school exams and university admissions.
