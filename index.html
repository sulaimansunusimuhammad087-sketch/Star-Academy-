About Star Academy

Star Academy is a premium community-based extra lesson program designed to rigorously prepare students for university admission. It runs every Saturday and Sunday from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM. We proudly train students from Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels, focusing intensely on Science and Arts departments to secure successful higher education paths.
