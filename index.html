About Star Academy

Star Academy is a premier community-based extra lesson program designed to rigorously prepare students for university admission and academic distinction. Operating every Saturday and Sunday from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, we train learners across Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels. Our core focus centers on mastery within the Science and Arts departments to secure successful higher education placement.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality academic support that equips students to pass examinations and gain university admission, while fostering strong academic discipline, integrity, and excellence.

Our Vision

To serve as a leading community educational center producing successful, high-achieving professionals in science, technology, arts, and the social sciences.
